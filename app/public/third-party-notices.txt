MOCsmith: third-party software in this page
=====================================================

The page ships three.js (0.170.0, including its addons GLTFLoader,
OrbitControls and BufferGeometryUtils). Making the instruction book's PDF
loads jsPDF (4.2.1) and the libraries it bundles: fflate, fast-png,
iobuffer, pako and helpers from @babel/runtime. The page is built with Vite,
which adds a small module-loading helper. All are under the MIT License
(pako also under the zlib licence); their notices are below. The page's
icons are copied from Lucide (lucide-static 1.55.0, ISC License, notice
below).

The mosaic page's 2.5D option (off by default) loads ONNX Runtime Web
(1.30.0, MIT License, notice below). Its WebAssembly build contains code
from other projects; Microsoft's list of those, with their licences, is in
onnxruntime-third-party-notices.txt next to this file (copied unchanged
from the onnxruntime v1.30.0 source). The source of that build, including
its MPL-2.0 parts such as Eigen, is at
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime/tree/v1.30.0 . The same option
makes the browser download the Depth Anything V2 Small depth model from
Hugging Face; the model is not part of this site (see the end of this
file).

Sample models, part and colour data are credited on the page's About
(#/about) and in samples/README.md of the source; each keeps its own licence.
