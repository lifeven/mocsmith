# MOCsmith product guide

MOCsmith is a browser tool for people who want to turn an existing 3D model into a brick design, adjust the result, and follow building instructions. A separate mosaic workflow turns photos into flat or raised brick pictures.

## 3D models

Choose a sample or open a `.glb`, `.ldr` or `.mpd` file. Adjust the size, colours and available part types. Compare the brick version with the original, then edit individual parts with undo and redo. The connectivity view marks parts that remain disconnected.

Start building to follow the automatic sequence. Steps can include chapters and subassemblies. Download a PDF instruction book, an LDraw model with step markers, or a CSV parts list.

## Photo mosaics

Choose a sample image or a local photo. Set the size, crop, colours, surface parts and base panels. Optionally match colours to a set inventory or add 2.5D relief. Download the mosaic as PDF, LDraw or a parts list.

## Availability and limits

The public app is free to use, with no account or payment flow. Public deployment status is recorded in [STATUS](STATUS.md). English and Chinese are supported.

Files are processed in the browser. The optional relief model is downloaded from Hugging Face and run locally. Set names and inventories are served by this site; official set photos are not requested. Structural force certification, manual editing of the step sequence, persistent project files and an image-to-3D service are not implemented.
