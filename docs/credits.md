# Data and sample credits

Third-party assets retain their own licences. The app’s About page also carries these credits.

## Data and software

Part numbers and colour references follow the [LDraw parts library](https://www.ldraw.org/) and LDConfig.ldr, under CC BY 2.0 / CC BY 4.0 as applicable. No LDraw part geometry is bundled. Part availability, set names and inventories derive from [Rebrickable data downloads](https://rebrickable.com/downloads/). Official set photographs are not included or requested.

Three.js and jsPDF are MIT-licensed; Lucide icons are ISC-licensed. Optional relief inference uses ONNX Runtime Web under MIT and [Depth Anything V2 Small](https://huggingface.co/depth-anything/Depth-Anything-V2-Small) by Lihe Yang and colleagues under Apache 2.0. The model is downloaded on demand rather than included here. Full dependency notices are in `app/public/third-party-notices.txt` and `app/public/onnxruntime-third-party-notices.txt`.

Algorithm and literature references, including legolization, lego-art-remix and Brickify, are retained in NOTICE. Their attribution is separate from each sample asset’s licence.
